# Haunted Mansion: art manifest

Generated by `tools/write_manifest.py` from `objects.py`, so sizes always match the game. Each sprite currently has a procedural placeholder (see `assets/art_reference/<name>.png` for its layout).

- Screen: 1280 x 800, drawn natively (no scaling).
- Floor tile: 112 x 56 px (2:1 isometric). Back walls 230 px tall. Archie and Holly are 160 px tall.
- Style: Painted, soft-shaded cartoon Halloween style matching Archie and Holly; rich but slightly muted colours, warm candlelight and cool moonlight; light from the upper left; transparent background (PNG with alpha), no drop shadow on the floor (the game adds one), no text.
- Adding art: Save a transparent PNG as assets/art/<name>.png. Any size: it is cropped to its opaque area and scaled (furniture to the footprint width, enemies to the target height, wall art to the exact target box). Run the game; real art replaces the placeholder automatically.
- Paint at roughly twice the target size (column *Paint at*); the game downsizes it with smooth scaling.

## Views

- **furniture**: Isometric 3/4 view from above (2:1 dimetric), front facing the viewer's lower RIGHT, back against the upper-left wall. Footprint diamond touches the bottom of the canvas. The game mirrors it for objects against the other wall.
- **flat**: Lies flat on the floor, same isometric view; footprint diamond fills the canvas width.
- **link**: Isometric, like furniture: front facing the lower right, back against the upper-left wall.
- **wall**: FLAT FRONT VIEW (not isometric), as if photographed straight on. The game squeezes and shears it onto the wall.
- **enemy**: Full figure, 3/4 view facing the viewer's lower RIGHT, feet (or lowest point) at the bottom of the canvas. The game mirrors it when the enemy moves left.
- **item**: Small item, 3/4 view, centred.
